Get in touch

BreathCheck is a small, independent project. If something feels broken or confusing, or if you simply want to share some feedback, I'd love to hear from you. The best way to get in touch is by email:

webshooterspiderman@gmail.com

Replies may not always be immediate, but every message is carefully read and appreciated.

What's especially useful to hear about

  • Something that felt broken, confusing, or difficult to use
  • A breathing technique or workday situation you'd like to see covered
  • Anything in the blog that felt unclear, inaccurate, or overstated
  • Ideas that could make BreathCheck more useful to you

A quick note: BreathCheck is a wellness and self-awareness tool, not a medical service. If you're experiencing breathing difficulties or have a health concern, please speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
